## Stream 1: Consumer Subscriptions

**Tiers:** Free / Basic ($3/mo) / Premium ($10/mo) / Elite ($20/mo)

- Annual discount: 17% (pay for 10 months, get 12)
- Year 5: 8M users, 50% paying, 4M subscribers
- Monthly churn improves from 6% (Y1) to 3% (Y5) with feature stickiness
- Tier mix evolves: Basic 26%, Premium 17%, Elite 7% by Y5

**Key Levers:** Feature gating (AI design, family cards, priority print), annual plan incentives

## Stream 2: Per-Card Issuance Fees

**9 Fee Types:**
1. New card issuance (physical): $3.50 — paid by bank/CU partner
2. Replacement (lost/stolen): $5.00 — split consumer/bank
3. Design change (physical): $2.50 — consumer pays
4. Digital card push (Apple/Google Pay): $1.00 — bank pays
5. Premium material (metal): $8.00 — consumer pays
6. Premium material (bamboo/custom): $12.00 — consumer pays
7. Gift card issuance (physical): $1.50 — retailer pays
8. Gift card issuance (digital): $0.50 — retailer pays
9. Emergency same-day reissue: $15.00 — consumer pays

**Key Insight:** Per-card fees flow regardless of subscription tier. Even free users generate $3.50+ per card for APODS when their bank orders the issuance.

## Stream 4: Gift Card Marketplace

**Digital-first marketplace** — zero shipping, zero inventory, instant delivery

- Exchange fee: 10% (seller pays, on 40% of GMV that is exchanges)
- Processing fee: 2.5% (buyer pays)
- New purchase fee: 3% (on 60% of GMV that is new purchases)
- Blended take rate: ~12.5% of GMV
- Y5 GMV: $2.28B, Revenue: $171M

**Why Digital Wins:** Instant delivery vs 7-14 days, no lost cards (stored in wallet), full exchange marketplace, zero waste, biometric-locked security option

2. Fiat-to-crypto purchase at kiosk: 2-4% fee (avg 3%)

- Assets: Multi-asset support (not just Bitcoin)
- On-ramp: Crypto loads directly to physical/digital card

**Y5:** $9.76B total crypto transaction volume, 1.88% blended take rate, $188M revenue

## Revenue Stream Interactions

The 8 streams create compounding network effects:

- **Users → Ads:** More users = more ad impressions at premium CPM
- **Kiosks → Licensing:** More deployed kiosks = more bank licensing revenue
- **Kiosks → Badges:** Same hardware serves banking AND badge markets
- **KYC → Crypto:** Existing identity verification enables instant crypto compliance
- **Gift Cards → B2B:** Retailer gift card listings drive B2B platform fees
- **Subscriptions → Cards:** Premium subscribers issue more cards = more per-card fees
- **Crypto → Subscriptions:** Crypto rewards card users are high-value Premium/Elite subscribers
- **Events → Advertising:** Conference badge issuance creates event-specific ad inventory
